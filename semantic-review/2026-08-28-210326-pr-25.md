# Atomic profile transactions across QOF-25 economy composites

QOF-25 introduces one exclusive transaction owner per loaded profile, makes composite currency spends silent until a single commit, and blocks persistence while compensation remains unresolved. The migrated paths cover Upgrade Tree, paid and free Egg flows, Shop, Potion upgrades, Auto-Hatch access, Machines, and Enchanting, with lifecycle retry through retained settlers. The two findings from the prior pass are resolved: Campaign SpecialEgg now uses the owner-backed EggService claim transaction, and Enchanting retains its complete projected Contract V1 response before the currency point of no return. Tests, generated-place parity, compilation, canonical packaging, and a fresh deterministic release build all pass.

**Watch for:** No confirmed QOF-25 issues remain. QOF-26 Potion/Shiny/AutoDrink lease races, QOF-27 machine input-removal-before-RNG ordering, and QOF-28 Auto-Hatch V2 remain explicitly deferred.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

`ProfileTransactionService` is the shared per-profile admission boundary. `DataService` closes the begin/save race before cloning a persistence snapshot, while leave and shutdown retain profile identity and retry unresolved settlers without allowing one blocked player to prevent independent profiles from saving.

`CurrencyService` reserves against a captured profile without changing its live balance or publishing an event. Composite services install domain-first compensation before mutation, construct their authoritative result before commit, and treat replication failures after the single debit as terminal presentation failures rather than retryable economic failures.

Campaign SpecialEgg claims route through `EggService.claimCampaignBossReward`. Pet insertion, canonical Dex discovery writes, and the exact `campaignBossRewards[tostring(levelNum)] = true` marker occur under one direct profile owner; campaign-specific publication omits normal hatch cinematics, Shiny-charge consumption, and hatch quest progress.

Enchanting projects the next revision and post-debit availability into the full Contract V1 DTO and retains it before committing currency. A successful paid roll returns that state even if later inventory replication or profile lookup fails.

The generated place contains all 79 runtime sources byte-for-byte, and the QOF-25 RBXL, ZIP, checksum manifest, and fresh rebuild match the documented hashes. The documentation keeps the Studio playtest outstanding rather than treating local verification as a substitute.

## Central ownership and persistence admission

```text
composite service
    -> silent CurrencyService reservation / direct free-reward owner
        -> ProfileTransactionService owner
            -> DataService snapshot gate
```

`ProfileTransactionService.begin` captures the exact Player and profile incarnation and rejects an existing owner, closed mutation admission, or an in-progress save (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/ProfileTransactionService.lua`). `DataService.savePlayerData` sets its save flag before checking `hasPending`, and owner creation checks that same flag, closing both sides of the begin/save race before `DataSchema.cloneForPersistence` (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/DataService.lua:190-206`). Autosave skips pending owners; departure and shutdown close admission, retry service settlement plus the central owner, and release only after settlement (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/DataService.lua:307-470`).

`CurrencyService.beginSpendTransaction` installs a default cancellation settler before returning, and each migrated composite replaces it before its first domain mutation (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/CurrencyService.lua:178-237`). Commit applies one exact debit to the then-current captured balance, releases the owner, and protects `CurrencyUpdated`; rollback cancels the reservation without a refund credit or reward multipliers (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/CurrencyService.lua:238-277`).

## Campaign SpecialEgg claim shares the Egg owner

`CampaignService._onVictory` delegates SpecialEgg handling to `EggService.claimCampaignBossReward` and no longer calls legacy `PetService.hatchEgg` (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/CampaignService.lua:528-556`). The claim method validates the canonical level key, bypasses station and zone checks for the server reward, disables price and Shiny-charge consumption, and passes the exact string key into the hatch transaction (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/EggService.lua:823-854`).

EggService acquires its direct profile owner before preparing or committing the free inventory mutation. `PetService.commitHatchBatch` writes the pet and canonical/compatibility Dex discovery keys under the held inventory lease, then EggService writes only `campaignBossRewards[claimKey] = true` under that same owner (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/PetService.lua:504-535`, `src/ServerScriptService/Services/EggService.lua:703-733`). Compensation restores the exact hostile or absent marker before the pet and discovery writes; failed pet restoration retains the same owner, lease, and transaction for cleanup retry (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/EggService.lua:389-480`).

The campaign notification path republishes inventory without emitting `EggHatchStart`, `EggHatchResult`, incrementing `hatchEggs`, or consuming Shiny charges (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/EggService.lua:554-563, 840-849`). Campaign clears the active battle before protected `CampaignVictory` transport, so a throwing client event cannot leave a replayable victory (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/CampaignService.lua:558-566`).

## Enchanting retains its Contract V1 response before PONR

Enchanting reserves Diamonds and installs pet-first compensation before RNG or mutation. After writing the rolled enchant, it builds the full Contract V1 state, projects `stateRevision + 1`, and adjusts availability from the exact post-debit balance before `commitSpendTransaction` (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/EnchantingService.lua:351-411`). Pet restoration precedes reservation cancellation and validates the exact profile, pets table, index, pet identity, and written enchant, leaving failed rollback owned and retryable.

The projected state is stored on the transaction and returned directly for every successful commit (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/EnchantingService.lua:394-401, 521-526`). Revision and inventory publication are protected postcommit work and cannot convert the paid result into a retry or erase its response.

## Composite transaction consistency

Upgrade Tree, Shop, Potion upgrades, Auto-Hatch access, Egg batches, Machines, and Enchanting all reserve through `beginSpendTransaction`, register a domain-first `setSpendSettler`, and avoid debit/refund rollback. Their precommit DTOs project the pending debit where currency affects availability; notifications are protected after commit; exact restoration is scoped to owned fields or table identities; and failed compensation remains retained locally or by the central settler.

Main wires the coordinator into CurrencyService and EggService, injects EggService into CampaignService, and includes central settlement in leave and shutdown checks (`src/ServerScriptService/Main.server.lua:210-245, 277-305, 989-1008`). The explicitly deferred QOF-26, QOF-27, and QOF-28 contracts are unchanged by this review.
