# Canonical unrounded combat damage across world and campaign paths

QOF-24 replaces staged integer flooring with one server-authoritative `base × enchant × quest × shop` calculation, preserving fractional damage through every consumer. Every finite positive factor, including `0.5`, participates; zero, negative, nonfinite, wrong-type, missing, or throwing providers become neutral, while a nonfinite final product fails closed to zero. Behavioral coverage now reaches world aggregation and overkill plus Campaign deploy HP, regular enemies, bosses, and the enemy base, and the generated release is bound to matching deterministic artifacts. Watch for: None in the reviewed diff; the documented Roblox Studio gate remains intentionally pending.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

`PetService.getPetDamage` remains the sole combat authority and never consults the replicated `pet.damage` mirror. Canonical variant/Shiny base damage and the whitelisted Strong enchant combine with runtime quest and shop factors without intermediate rounding; provider failures cannot inject malformed values into combat (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/PetService.lua:63-69`, `src/ServerScriptService/Services/PetService.lua:1331-1378`).

ZoneService sums authoritative per-pet values before clamping once to remaining HP. Its behavioral spec reaches `attackDestructible` and demonstrates both `4.5 + 4.5 = 9` and the 8-HP contribution boundary (`tests/ZoneService.spec.lua:103-151`; `src/ServerScriptService/Services/ZoneService.lua:2452-2538`).

Campaign deployment snapshots fractional damage and derives HP from it, while attacks resolve centralized damage for ordinary enemies, bosses, and the enemy base. The new tests exercise those production entry points (`tests/CampaignService.spec.lua:124-212`; `src/ServerScriptService/Services/CampaignService.lua:219-263`, `src/ServerScriptService/Services/CampaignService.lua:304-404`).

The generated-place verifier is backed by byte-exact 78-source parity, and the XML place, binary place, canonical ZIP, provenance manifest, and fresh rebuild match. Documentation and roadmap consistently retain the pending Studio gate (`tests/verify_generated_place.py:639-692`, `docs/QOF-24-canonical-damage-precision.md:1-102`, `QOF-22-31-ROADMAP-README.md:156`).

## One authoritative base × enchant × quest × shop chain

The factor normalizer accepts exactly finite values greater than zero, preserving reductions such as `0.5` (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/PetService.lua:63-69`). `getPetDamage` resolves canonical base damage independently of the compatibility mirror, protects each provider, multiplies the four factors once, and fails a nonfinite complete result to zero (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/PetService.lua:1331-1378`).

Tests cover all six Normal/Golden/Rainbow and Shiny combinations, every Strong tier over a `4.5` base, the unrounded factor chain, reductions from both runtime providers, malformed and throwing providers, forged `pet.damage`, overflow, and the changed precision expectation from `9` to `11.25` (`tests/PetService.spec.lua:168-282`, `tests/PetService.spec.lua:590-606`).

## Aggregate once, then clamp world overkill

The ZoneService spec installs two equipped pets against a real target and calls the public attack method. One case leaves a 10-HP target at 1 and records/emits 9; the other records exactly 8 against an 8-HP target and enters the destruction branch (`tests/ZoneService.spec.lua:103-151`). Only the finalizer and event emitter are replaced as observation seams; aggregation, authorization, contribution clamping, HP mutation, and branch selection remain production behavior.

## Fractional Campaign state and all outgoing attack targets

The production deploy path preserves `damage = 4.5` and derives `hp = maxHp = 22.5` (`tests/CampaignService.spec.lua:145-171`). Update-loop cases assert exact `4.5` subtraction for regular and boss enemies and the enemy base (`tests/CampaignService.spec.lua:173-211`). Boss status shares the same attack arithmetic, so both flag values protect that common path.
