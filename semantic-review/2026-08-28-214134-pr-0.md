# Shared Potion concurrency and lifecycle ownership for QOF-26

QOF-26 replaces independent Potion mutation locks with one exact per-player lease shared by Hatch Shiny reservations, direct Potion actions, upgrades, reconciliation, and Potion Shop purchases. Direct Potion paths additionally participate in the central save-aware profile owner, while Egg and Shop reuse existing currency/profile ownership and nest only the Potion-domain lease. Egg alone settles its Shiny handle, with additive rollback before the currency point of no return and retained commit retry afterward. The prior stale generated-place finding is resolved: all 79 runtime sources now have byte-exact parity with `BATTLE_PETS.rbxlx`, and no QOF-26 behavioral issue remains.

**Watch for:** No confirmed QOF-26 concerns remain. QOF-27 machine ordering and QOF-28 Auto-Hatch V2 are deliberately outside this review.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

One opaque Potion lease serializes every path that can alter Potion inventory, active effects, Shiny charges, selection, upgrade state, or Potion-facing revisions. Hatch retains that exact lease through settlement, so competitors reject with `BUSY` before observing or overwriting intermediate charge state.

Direct consume, Auto-Drink, selection, reconciliation, join initialization, and standalone inventory publication acquire the central QOF-25 owner as well as the Potion lease. Egg and Shop do not attempt a second central owner: their currency transaction already owns the profile boundary while the nested lease protects the Potion domain.

Shiny rollback restores only the handle's reserved count and refuses to clamp or discard an unresolved restoration. Egg remains the exclusive handle settler, and Main gates Potion cleanup on successful Egg settlement during leave and shutdown, preserving post-PONR commit retry.

Shutdown closes Egg, Shop, and Potion admission before settlement. DataService takes the releasing save only after domain settlement, central profile settlement, and both Pet and Potion idle checks succeed; unresolved work retains the cached profile for retry.

The acceptance evidence covers real Egg/Potion/Shop contention at 5, 29, and 30 charges, pre-PONR restoration, post-PONR retry, save/leave and shutdown admission, exact lease identity, and full-fit Shiny drinking. Existing tests retain Luck, Mega Luck, Speed, Coin Boost, duration, slots, and Auto-Drink coverage; the full suite passes 409/409 in normal and reverse order.

## Exact Potion lease across direct and composite mutations

Hatch reservation, manual consumption, Auto-Drink, upgrades, selection, reconciliation, Shop Potion purchases, and lifecycle checks converge on the exact-identity lease registry (`src/ServerScriptService/Services/PotionService.lua:339-447`). A Hatch subtracts its reservation while retaining the lease, so every competing path returns `BUSY` before currency work, inventory mutation, revision changes, or events.

Direct mutations take the local lease and then `ProfileTransactionService` ownership before profile mutation; failed central acquisition releases the local lease (`PotionService.lua:389-419`). Paid Egg flows already acquire central ownership through `beginSpendTransaction` before reserving Shiny charges (`EggService.lua:630-687`), while Shop takes the Potion lease before its currency transaction (`ShopService.lua:493-520`). This nested contract prevents recursive central ownership while retaining both save and Potion-domain exclusion.

## Additive Shiny restoration and full-fit consumption

A Shiny reservation stores its captured profile, active-buff table, retained lease, and reserved count. Rollback validates those identities, adds only that count, and refuses settlement if the result would exceed the cap (`PotionService.lua:760-780`); it neither restores a stale snapshot nor clamps, so 5/29/30 conservation and retry remain exact.

Consumption rejects a Shiny Potion unless its complete three-charge grant fits under the 30-charge cap (`PotionService.lua:195-226`). Auto-Drink considers Shiny only when no positive charges remain. Regression coverage rejects 29-plus-3 without inventory or charge mutation and accepts the exact 27-to-30 fit.

## Egg-exclusive settlement through leave and shutdown

Egg rollback invokes Shiny rollback before releasing currency ownership, while committed Egg settlement invokes only Shiny commit (`EggService.lua:414-503`). Potion cleanup never chooses a pending handle's outcome; it reports unresolved until Egg removes it (`PotionService.lua:873-890`). A technical precommit failure therefore restores the reservation, while a paid Hatch whose Shiny finalization transiently fails retains its successful pets, debit, handle, and lease until commit retry publishes one Potion revision.

Main applies the same order during shutdown and player removal: Egg settles first, Shop settles its independent owner, Potion cleanup runs only after Egg succeeds, and DataService receives success only after central settlement plus Pet and Potion idle checks (`src/ServerScriptService/Main.server.lua:288-315, 991-1021`). Existing handles remain settleable after new admission closes.

## Conservation, regression, and generated-place evidence

`tests/QOF26PotionLifecycle.spec.lua` uses the real EggService, PotionService, and ShopService for three-charge reservations at 5, 29, and 30. It covers competing direct and composite paths, exact rollback, one-revision commit, post-PONR retry, failed additive restoration retention, save/leave admission, exact-handle release, and shutdown settlement.

The broader Potion and Shop suites cover timed-source extension, Luck/Mega Luck coexistence, Speed refresh, Coin Boost, slot and duration upgrades, Auto-Drink, purchase rollback, and retained lifecycle ownership.
